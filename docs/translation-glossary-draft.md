# Contextual glossary draft

For bilingual/domain review, not authoritative legal terminology. These entries are candidate equivalents, not unconditional search-and-replace rules. English-to-Urdu and Urdu-to-English need separate contextual review.

| English | Urdu candidate | Applicable context | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| applicant | درخواست گزار | Person making an application | Preserve identity and role |
| application | درخواست | Administrative request | Not a software application |
| party | فریق | Person/entity party to an agreement | Not political party or celebration |
| parties | فریقین | Two parties | Check number of parties; do not force dual form for many parties |
| receipt | رسید | A receipt document | Receipt of payment may instead require وصولی |
| payment | ادائیگی | Money paid | Preserve who pays whom |
| approval | منظوری | Administrative approval | Requesting approval does not mean approval was granted |
| written consent | تحریری رضامندی | Agreement given in writing | Do not omit written requirement |
| subject to approval | منظوری سے مشروط | Conditional action | Never turn into unconditional approval |
| tenant | کرایہ دار | Tenancy context | Do not swap with landlord |
| landlord | مالک مکان | House tenancy context | Other property contexts may require different wording |
| affidavit | حلف نامہ | Name of document type | Translating the term does not create or certify an affidavit |
| presently serving as | فی الحال بطور … خدمات انجام دے رہا/رہی ہوں | Employment/service in a role | Never translate service as residence or add مقیم |
| CNIC | کمپیوٹرائزڈ قومی شناختی کارڈ (CNIC) | Pakistani computerized national identity card | Retain CNIC; do not drop computerized specificity |
| No Objection Certificate (NOC) | عدم اعتراض سرٹیفکیٹ (NOC) | Certificate name | Retain NOC, including subsequent references |
| rules, regulations, and conditions | قواعد، ضوابط اور شرائط | Scope of an undertaking | Do not add قوانین/laws where absent |
| material information / material fact | اہم معلومات / اہم حقیقت | Information significant to the declaration | Material here is not a physical substance; specialist review remains needed |
| to the best of my knowledge and belief | میرے علم اور یقین کے مطابق | Qualification of a declaration | Do not remove the qualification or strengthen certainty |
